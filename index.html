Introduction to Computational Biology

A first-year guide to how biology and computing meet: from the cell, to proteins, to AI in healthcare. Five units, thirty topics, explained simply.

Code 26BTB1001TDiscipline course (B/E/C)L2 T0 P0 C2Offered by BiotechnologyNo prerequisites

Unit 1: Cell Basics and Computing Analogies

The cell is the smallest working unit of life. Here we compare it to things you already know from computing.

Unit 2: Biomolecules as Data Structures

Life is built from four main molecule classes. We treat them as storage, structure and information formats.

Unit 3: Protein Structures and Prediction Algorithms

How a sequence becomes a shape, and how computers predict that shape.

Unit 4: AI/ML Foundations in Biology

Learn the machine learning basics needed to work with biological data.

Unit 5: Deep Learning, Healthcare and Ethics

Neural networks in medicine, and the responsibilities that come with them.

Assessment. CLA-1 covers Units 1 and 2. CLA-2 covers Units 3 to 5. Each unit has 6 lecture sessions (S1 to S6).
